Data Scientist (all genders) – Vienna

About us
Lobau Grid Analytics was founded in 2019 and has grown to 60 people from 18 countries. We help regional energy
suppliers in Austria, Slovenia and Slovakia to plan their power grids: where will the next heat pumps and charging
stations appear, and which cables and transformers will run hot first? Our customers use the answers to decide where to
invest several million euros every year.

We are proud of our engineering culture. Our platform team runs everything on Kubernetes and streams meter data
through Kafka, our web front end is written in React, and every change goes through a code review. Once a month the
whole company stops normal work for a hack day, and the best ideas often end up in the product. We publish our
research, give talks at conferences about energy systems and machine learning, and support two open-source projects.

Our office is in a former tram depot next to the Danube, five minutes from the underground station. We have a roof
terrace, a workshop for bikes and a kitchen where someone cooks lunch twice a week.
